Five kinds of board game

Every game on this site sits in one of these five families. If you only remember one thing, remember these.

Family

Easy rules, no reading required, works with people who don't play games.

12 games
Party

Loud, fast, six or more people. Nobody sits and thinks for five minutes.

10 games
Strategy

Long games with real decisions. The ones people write spreadsheets about.

14 games
Thematic

You're a character in a story. Dice, monsters, betrayal, campaigns.

Pure puzzle, no story. Chess's modern descendants — beautiful and mean.

They're here because each one does something the others don't, and I can tell you exactly what that thing is. Some are popular, some aren't; plenty of the games everyone lists are just good games having a very good decade.
